The city's bike-sharing scheme added two hundred electric bicycles this month. The visually impaired students asked for exam papers in Braille and large print. My phone's map app took us down a narrow road that ended at a river. The online form rejected my name because it contained an apostrophe. The museum's virtual tour was more popular than its real one during the lockdown. Nobody could explain why the office printer only worked on Wednesdays. The small bakery posts its daily menu on a messaging channel every morning at seven. The research group shared its dataset publicly so other teams could repeat the experiments. The headphones cancel noise so well that I missed my stop twice.
